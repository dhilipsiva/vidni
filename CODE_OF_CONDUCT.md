# Code of conduct

vidni is made in the open, and anyone is welcome to use it, ask about it and help with it. This page says what is expected of everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is talked about.

## What is expected

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Most questions are honest and most mistakes are honest mistakes.
- **Talk about the work, not the person.** Say what is wrong with a change and why; never what is wrong with whoever made it.
- **Leave room for others.** People come with different experience, first languages and amounts of time. Explain what you know without talking down.
- **Take a no gracefully, and give one clearly.** A maintainer may decline a change that is good; say why, and accept it when it is said to you.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, mockery or personal attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including unwelcome sexual attention.
- Demeaning anyone for who they are: their sex or gender, sexual orientation, age, disability, appearance, race, ethnicity, nationality, caste, religion, or level of experience.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Keeping on after being asked to stop.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that go against this page, and may block a person from the project for a time or for good. The response fits what happened: a word in private for a first lapse, a ban for harassment.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour goes against this page, tell the maintainer in private: his contact is on [his GitHub profile](https://github.com/dhilipsiva). Say what happened, where, and when; a link or a screenshot helps. A report is read by the maintainer only, and nobody is told who made it.

If the report is about the maintainer, [GitHub's own reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) is the place.
